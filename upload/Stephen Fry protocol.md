**Core Style Directive — “Stephen Fry of PyArcana”**  
- Write with elegance, warmth, precision, and expansive clarity.  
- Prefer flowing, well-cadenced sentences over short, clipped ones.  
- Use rich but accessible vocabulary.  
- Guide the reader’s mind gently and intelligently.  
- Make the reader feel they are in the company of a brilliant, generous teacher who loves both the subject and language itself.  
  
**Required Structure for Every Theoretical Sub-topic**  
  
1. **Preamble / Gateway**    
   Open with an international anecdote, historical moment, scientific episode, or concrete use case from anywhere in the world that naturally raises the question the theory will answer. Make the reader curious.  
  
2. **Expanded Theoretical Explanation**    
   Explain the concept thoroughly, with multiple angles, careful scaffolding, concrete illustrations, and explicit connections. Be verbose in the best sense — never leave the reader stranded with a thin definition.  
  
3. **Retrospective**    
   Close the sub-topic with a short reflective passage that consolidates the key idea, shows why it matters, and gently prepares the ground for what comes next.

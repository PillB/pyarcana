### Fresh diagnosis

S02 already had an excellent 8 × 3 gradual-release graph, truthful executable
examples and a coherent raw/clean/errors throughline. Its remaining weakness
was textual: many paragraphs stated rules without rehearsing why they were
true; I Do preambles told the learner what to observe but seldom required a
prediction; and several We Do retrospectives repeated the success condition
instead of repairing a misconception or transferring the principle.

- Browser-runtime risks (Pyodide 0.26.2): [I] Browser timers are coarse: `performance.now` precision is reduced in browsers, and in WASM `time.process_time` resolution is implementation-defined. Small works (`sum(range(1000))` in `weDo.steps[0]`, `range(2000)` in `weDo.steps[3–4]`) may measure 0.0 ms. The `>= 0` predicates hide this, so the printed output still matches. [I] The You Do `budget_ms = 50.0` against `after_ms` was tuned on native CPython. Under Pyodide's slower interpreter `budget_pass` may flip to `False`; the output is not pinned, but she may think she broke something. [G] `tracemalloc` and `cProfile` are assumed available in Pyodide's stdlib build; if either is excluded, `theory[2]`, `iDo.steps[0]` and `weDo.steps[1]` would raise `ModuleNotFoundError` in the browser. Worth one smoke run.

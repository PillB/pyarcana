### S15-F01 — Learner can expect row concat to reconcile duplicate business labels and silently build wrong reconciliation logic.
- **Category:** INDEX_CONCAT_FALSE_MENTAL_MODEL
- **Location:** opening p2
- **Evidence:** Opening says Series concatenated by rows align by Index. pd.concat(axis=0) stacks along the index axis and can preserve duplicate labels; it does not reconcile labels like Series arithmetic.
- **Impact:** Learner can expect row concat to reconcile duplicate business labels and silently build wrong reconciliation logic.
- **Proposed fix:** Separate Series arithmetic alignment, concat(axis=0) stacking and concat(axis=1) alignment.

### S30-F05 — EMAIL_LOCAL_PART_CASE_COLLAPSE
- **Location:** T1-A comparators.py / I Do exact email
- **Evidence:** exact() casefolds the entire email; RFC 5321 requires preserving/treating mailbox local-part case sensitivity while domain is case-insensitive, even though case-sensitive mailboxes are discouraged.

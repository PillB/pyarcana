### S18-F03 — A learner can use mean-minus-median as a tail detector without inspecting the distribution and can overstate median invariance.
- **Category:** MEAN_MEDIAN_SEPARATION_OVERCLAIMS_TAIL
- **Location:** opening.p2
- **Evidence:** The paragraph states that a large mean-median separation is itself a finding that 'la distribución tiene cola' and says the median 'no se inmuta' before one extreme. Separation can suggest skew/outliers/mixtures but does not uniquely diagnose a tail, and a changed observation can affect the median if it crosses ranks near the center.
- **Impact:** A learner can use mean-minus-median as a tail detector without inspecting the distribution and can overstate median invariance.
- **Proposed fix:** Say the separation is a signal to inspect skewness, outliers or mixtures; describe median as resistant, not immovable.

# Distilled writing rules

Every rule below is checkable against a passage, and cites where it comes from. The
full sources are in the repository and are the authority when this file is ambiguous:

- `ReviewerFixer/Solarized Website Content Comprehension Auditor & Rewriter.md` — *(S)*
- `ReviewerFixer/PYARCANA — MODO REVISOR ESCÉPTICO Y ANTI-COMPLACENCIA.md` — *(E)*
- `ReviewerFixer/_GRAMMAR_SUBPLAN.md` — *(G)*
- `Handcrafted Writing and Editorial Quality Protocol.docx` — *(H)*

This file is distilled on purpose. 49 KB of contract pasted into a prompt gets
skimmed; a numbered rule you can hold a sentence against does not.

## A. Terms

- **A1 — No widow terms.** A term a learner has not met is defined at first use, in
  the same sentence or the next. This binds in taglines and learning outcomes too,
  not only in theory. *(S §2, D1)*
- **A2 — Explained is not understood.** A definition that restates the label
  ("un validador valida") teaches nothing. Say what it *is*, in words already
  available to the reader. *(E §7)*
- **A3 — Expand an acronym at first use**, then use the short form consistently. *(S §8H)*
- **A4 — No internal vocabulary as if it were ordinary language.** Gate, intake,
  pipeline, artefact and CP-N*-* ids are house terms; introduce them. *(S §8I)*
- **A5 — One name per concept.** Do not alternate synonyms for the same thing. *(S §5)*

## B. Explanation

- **B1 — Relationships, not just definitions.** Say what acts on what, in which
  direction, and what results. A list of definitions is not an explanation. *(S §5)*
- **B2 — Causal and concrete beats abstract.** Prefer a worked situation with real
  values over a general statement. *(E §9, S §4)*
- **B3 — Every meaningful element must stand on its own.** A callout, figure caption
  or exercise read in isolation should still make sense. *(S §6)*
- **B4 — Name subject, source, destination, action, purpose and consequence.** A
  passage missing any of these leaves a question the reader cannot answer. *(S §8B–G)*
- **B5 — Avoid nominalisation and excessive abstraction.** "La validación del registro
  produce el rechazo" → "el validador rechaza el registro". *(S §8J–K)*
- **B6 — Orient before explaining.** Where am I, why does this exist, what does it
  address, how does it relate to what came before. *(H §2A)*

## C. Repair

- **C1 — Repair the defect, not the paragraph.** Do not rewrite more than the finding
  requires. Voice, structure and working material stay. *(E §13)*
- **C2 — Suspicion is not a verdict.** Attack, then defend, then decide. A finding
  that survives only the attack is not a finding. *(E §5)*
- **C3 — "No change" carries the burden of proof.** Leaving a passage alone is a claim
  that a beginner succeeds with it. *(E §6)*
- **C4 — Defect, not preference.** A different wording you prefer is not a defect.
  Name the learner failure it causes, or drop it. *(E §12)*
- **C5 — Say how the learner fails.** Every finding states what the reader does wrong
  because of this passage. *(E §11)*
- **C6 — Never invent support.** No benchmark, version, guarantee or citation the
  section cannot back. If evidence is missing, raise a question instead. *(E §2)*

## D. Spanish sentence and paragraph mechanics *(all from G §C)*

- **D1 — Sentence length.** Over 32 words is long, over 45 is a run-on: split it.
- **D2 — Paragraphs are 2–4 sentences**, one focus each. A paragraph that is a single
  long sentence has not been segmented pedagogically.
- **D3 — Paired Spanish marks.** `¿…?` and `¡…!` open and close. Never the English
  half-form.
- **D4 — Balanced delimiters** `() [] «» ""`.
- **D5 — Terminal punctuation** on every sentence.
- **D6 — No doubled words** (`de de`), no space before punctuation, no double spaces.
- **D7 — No English-dominant sentences.** English survives only inside `code`.
- **D8 — Fewer than three gerunds in a row**; prefer finite verbs.
- **D9 — Vary sentence openings.** Repeated openers read as template rhythm.
- **D10 — Watch comma density.** Heavy hypotaxis becomes a list or shorter clauses.
- **D11 — Check gender and number agreement** on determiner–noun pairs.
- **D12 — No authoring residue.** No TODO, no meta commentary, no model voice: the
  register is a teacher speaking to a learner. *(also H §1)*

## E. Readability targets *(G §A)*

Fernández-Huerta `206.84 − 60·(sílabas/palabra) − 1.02·(palabras/oración)`.
For technical curriculum, roughly **50–70** is healthy. Much higher can mean
under-teaching; much lower means cognitive overload. Treat as a signal for ranking
passages, never as proof that prose is good.

## F. Scale

- **F1 — Size never lowers per-passage attention.** Writing is a sequence of units
  each understood and reviewed on its own, not a bulk production problem. *(H §1)*
- **F2 — Preserve what works.** Existing correct material, examples and voice are
  valuable until shown otherwise. *(E §13, AGENTS.md MUST 1)*

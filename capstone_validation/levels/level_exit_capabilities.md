# Level Exit Capabilities (observable, non-inflated)

## L1 — Fundamentos Guiados (S01–S13)
**Exit capability:** follow, modify, test and explain bounded data-processing programs with support.
Observed by CP-N1-A/B/C. Does NOT establish workplace seniority or licensure.

## L2 — Práctica Aplicada Independiente (S14–S26)
**Exit capability:** complete defined analytical, reporting and automation work using established methods and explicit review gates.
Observed by CP-N2-A/B/C. Does NOT certify a workplace title or employability.

## L3 — Integración y Evaluación Avanzada (S27–S39)
**Exit capability:** design and evaluate ER, graph, data and ML workflows under ambiguity, uncertainty and human review.
Observed by CP-N3-A/B/C. Does NOT constitute senior/staff/expert organisational level.

## L4 — Sistemas de Producción Gobernados (S40–S52)
**Exit capability:** integrate, deploy, observe, secure and defend a bounded production-style data and AI system.
Observed by CP-N4-A/B/C + CP-FINAL. Does NOT grant architect/master/external certification.

Scaffolding decreases across levels: heavy I Do/We Do in L1; independent You Do in L2;
design under ambiguity in L3; governed production with minimal scaffolding in L4.

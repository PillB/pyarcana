# Migration plan — CP-N4-C v1 → v2

> Status: executed.
> Date: 2026-07-30.
> Governing spec: Section 9 (CP-N4-C), Section 11 (Backward compatibility).
> Source of truth: `src/data/capstones.ts` (`CP_N4_C` at v2.0.0),
> `src/data/levels.ts` (`CARDINALITY.cpN4CSubGates`).

## Goal

Fold the requested production multi-agent project into `CP-N4-C` without
breaking the 13-capstone invariant, the stable IDs, the rubric registry, the
badge registry, the section mappings, or the final-integration contracts.

## Non-goals

- Creating a `CP-N4-D` principal capstone (rejected — see
  `ADR-N4-C-agentic-harness.md`).
- Renaming `CP-N4-C`, its badge `B-N4-C`, or its rubric `RUBRICS["CP-N4-C"]`.
- Changing the principal gate section `S51`.

## Steps (executed)

1. **Preserve the capstone ID.** `CP-N4-C.capstoneId` remains `"CP-N4-C"`.
2. **Preserve the gate section.** `CP-N4-C.gateSection` remains `"S51"`.
3. **Bump the version.** `CP-N4-C.version` goes from `1.x` to `"2.0.0"`.
4. **Add the three sub-gates.** `CP-N4-C.subGates` now contains:
   - `{ id: "CP-N4-C.1", sectionId: "S49", title: "Harness, adapters, RAG, tools, web/SERP, budget, approval", scope: [...] }`
   - `{ id: "CP-N4-C.2", sectionId: "S50", title: "Evaluation, red-team, reliability and recovery", scope: [...] }`
   - `{ id: "CP-N4-C.3", sectionId: "S51", title: "Observability, governance, incident-response, UX and final CP-N4-C gate", scope: [...] }`
5. **Keep S51 as principal gate.** `CP-N4-C.3 · S51` is the final sub-gate;
   `CP-N4-C.gateSection === "S51"` is unchanged.
6. **Update the rubric.** `RUBRICS["CP-N4-C"]` is at `version: "2.0.0"` and adds
   five critical criteria: `BL`, `CI`, `HR`, `TR`, `EV` (plus the no-key/no-chatbot
   critical failures).
7. **Add the runnable harness.** `src/lib/copilot-harness.ts` ships the
   provider-neutral adapters, RAG with access filtering, narrow tools with
   allowlists, generator–verifier, budget enforcement, approval gate, redaction,
   and OTel-style traces.
8. **Register the sub-gates in the cardinality invariant.**
   `CARDINALITY.cpN4CSubGates` carries the three sub-gate IDs and sections; the
   invariant's `n4dDecision` is `"fold_into_n4c"`.
9. **Mirror the change.** `capstone_validation/` is regenerated by
   `scripts/mirror.mjs`; the ledger shows `CP-N4-C` at `v2.0.0` with
   `subGateIds: ["CP-N4-C.1", "CP-N4-C.2", "CP-N4-C.3"]`.

## Backward compatibility

- `capstoneId`, `gateSection`, badge id, rubric id — **unchanged**.
- `version` is the only field bumped on the contract header.
- Learner progress stored in `localStorage` (keyed by `capstoneId`) is
  preserved: the same `CP-N4-C` key continues to resolve.
- The final-integration interface `copilot.run` is unchanged:
  `POST /copilot/v1/run { task, provider_mode, budget } → { run_id, steps, citations, trace, approval_status }`.
- The *Backward compatibility* test group in `tests/capstones.test.ts` asserts
  every stable ID and that `CP-N4-C` is preserved (not renamed to `CP-N4-D`).
